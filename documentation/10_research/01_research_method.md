# Research Method

## Purpose

This project should be developed as an empirical research effort rather than a collection of unsupported architectural claims.

## For each major idea, record

```text
Hypothesis
Why we believe it
Prior research
Implementation
Evaluation
Failure cases
Unexpected results
Decision
Next experiment
```

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer:

1. reproducible experiments,
2. primary research papers,
3. official documentation,
4. benchmark results,
5. carefully interpreted secondary analysis.

## Important distinction

A feature being plausible does not mean it is proven effective.

Documentation should explicitly distinguish:

- established result,
- research hypothesis,
- engineering assumption,
- observed project result.
